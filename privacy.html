Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This application ("MLBB Highlights Pipeline") is a personal content-production tool. This page describes what data it handles.

What data is processed

The app processes Mobile Legends: Bang Bang gameplay video recordings belonging to the operator, in order to produce highlight videos for publishing.

Platform authorization

To publish videos, the app uses OAuth access/refresh tokens issued by YouTube and TikTok, granted directly by the operator's own account. These tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device and are not transmitted to any third party or external service.

No data sale or sharing

This app does not collect data from, or about, any third-party users. No data is sold or shared with external parties.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the operator via the GitHub repository associated with this project.
